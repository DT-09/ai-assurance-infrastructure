The system

One control layer across the AI lifecycle.

AAI sits above models, tools, agents and infrastructure. It turns assurance evidence into durable identity, trust state and enforceable decisions.

01 · IdentityRegister AI assets, versions, owners, environments and capabilities.
02 · DependenciesMap models, tools, services, credentials and external dependencies.
03 · EvidenceBind evaluations and provenance to exact asset versions.
04 · TrustCompute explainable, persistent assurance state and transitions.
05 · ControlAuthorize deployment and runtime execution using policy.
Infrastructure primitive

Not a single pass/fail test.

Assurance is a continuous control loop. A dependency or capability change can invalidate evidence, trigger impact analysis and change runtime authority.
