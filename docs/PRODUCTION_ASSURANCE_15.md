# AAI Production Assurance v15

## Commercial product

AAI is sold initially as a **$10,000 fixed-scope production assurance sprint** for one consequential AI workflow. Payment unlocks the private customer workspace and control-plane APIs. Public protocol, documentation and evaluation surfaces remain accessible.

## Customer outcome

AAI must answer four operational questions:

1. What AI exists and what authority does it possess?
2. What did it actually do in production?
3. Which observed behavior violates a requirement, policy or authority boundary?
4. What evidence proves the decision and what control should change?

## Required production path

`Discover → Identity → Authority → Trace → Evaluate → Find → Risk → Policy → Decide → Enforce → Evidence → Release → Monitor → Change → Re-assure → Incident → Remediate → Verify`

## Included in the $10K sprint

- AI asset and dependency discovery
- Agent identity and delegated-authority mapping
- Model/tool/API/data boundary mapping
- Production trace ingestion and correlation
- Adversarial assurance planning and execution surface
- Prompt injection and indirect injection testing
- Tool misuse and unauthorized-action testing
- Data egress and tenant-isolation testing
- MCP/tool poisoning and dependency testing
- Memory/context poisoning checks
- Reliability, recovery and failure-path evaluation
- Deterministic risk and policy decisioning
- Runtime authorization and deny/review/allow controls
- Human approval checkpoints
- Tamper-evident evidence and provenance
- Deployment/release gate
- Change-impact analysis and reassessment triggers
- Incident and remediation lifecycle
- Machine-verifiable Assurance Passport
- Final evidence package and implementation handoff

## Explicit scope

The $10K product is one workflow. It is not a promise that every enterprise connector, private deployment, SSO configuration, or fleet-wide integration is implemented inside the fixed fee. Those are expansion scopes after the initial production engagement.

## Payment boundary

Production control-plane access is disabled until a verified payment entitlement is active. Entitlement is issued server-side from the payment event; client-side flags cannot unlock production access. Development/test environments may bypass the gate only when explicitly configured.
