# Go-to-Market: AI Agent Authority Review

## Positioning

**Headline:** Know whether your AI agent stayed inside its authority.

**One-line offer:** A 60-minute, evidence-backed review that compares an agent's declared tools, actions, data boundaries and approval rules with its actual execution traces.

Do not position AAI as an AI safety certification company, a general governance platform, or a universal security scanner.

## Initial segment

Start with AI automation agencies and B2B SaaS vendors deploying customer-support and order-management agents with CRM/order tools. These operators have repeated client handoffs and release changes. Exclude high-impact and regulated workflows from the first offer.

## Buyer and buying trigger

- Economic buyer: agency founder, CTO, VP Engineering or delivery director.
- Technical champion: AI engineer, platform engineer or agent developer.
- Trigger: production release, client handoff, tool/permission change, unexpected action or customer assurance request.
- Urgency test: the buyer must have a release date, observed exception or customer requirement within 30 days.

## Channel strategy

1. **Developer discovery:** publish the local CLI, synthetic fixture and technical method on GitHub. Make the report format inspectable.
2. **Search intent:** publish focused pages on agent authority drift, tool-permission testing, approval bypass evidence and AI agent release checks. Each page should lead to the same paid review.
3. **Partner distribution:** approach a small set of AI automation agencies, agent implementation consultancies and fractional CTOs with a concrete referral proposition. Do not mass-email lists.
4. **Technical communities:** publish the synthetic findings and reproducible CLI demo in relevant developer and agent-builder communities, following their promotion rules.
5. **Proof loop:** turn anonymized, permissioned, buyer-approved findings into case studies. Never invent customer names, savings or incident outcomes.

## Sales process

1. Qualify the trigger: one agent, one version, one release or incident, and a named decision owner.
2. Confirm the buyer can provide sanitized traces and an authority contract.
3. Confirm the workflow is outside excluded high-impact domains.
4. Send the fixed scope and fee before any data transfer.
5. Run the local CLI; do not ask the buyer to upload raw traces to a public website.
6. Review findings with the buyer and record accepted, rejected and unresolved findings.
7. Ask whether the result changes the release decision and whether a repeat review is needed after the next material change.

## Sales qualification

Disqualify if the buyer:
- Has no real agent or no tool/action traces.
- Cannot define allowed actions or tools.
- Wants a certificate or guarantee.
- Cannot authorize the data review.
- Expects live production changes or incident response.
- Has no owner, release trigger or budget.

## Funnel metrics

Track weekly:
- Qualified target accounts reached
- Replies from actual decision-makers
- Buyers with a near-term release or incident
- Sanitized evidence packages received
- Paid reviews booked and paid
- Reports accepted as useful
- Repeat purchase rate
- Delivery time per review
- Gross revenue per delivery hour

The target is at least US$100 gross revenue per delivery hour. Count preparation, analysis, meeting and follow-up time; do not count only the meeting hour.

## 30-day execution

### Days 1–3
- Run the synthetic fixture and verify the report.
- Publish the CLI and exact limitations.
- Replace broad $10,000 positioning with the narrow offer.
- Prepare one synthetic case study, clearly labelled synthetic.

### Days 4–10
- Build a list of 20 agencies/vendors with public evidence that they deploy tool-using agents.
- Prioritize those with customer-support, CRM or order-management agents and public release activity.
- Seek warm introductions and targeted partner conversations; no mass outreach.
- Goal: 5 qualified conversations, not vanity traffic.

### Days 11–20
- Convert the first qualified buyer into a paid $179 review.
- Deliver using the fixed input schema and report.
- Measure actual time, usefulness and buyer action.
- Do not discount to zero or perform open-ended free audits.

### Days 21–30
- Seek a second paid review and one repeat engagement.
- Publish a case study only with written permission and anonymization.
- Continue only if buyers pay and the findings change real decisions.

## Economics

At US$179 for a 60-minute review, the target is not automatically met. If total delivery time is 90 minutes, gross revenue is about US$119 per delivery hour before payment fees, tax and other costs. If total delivery time exceeds 107 minutes, the gross rate falls below US$100/hour.

The offer must therefore be time-boxed, standardized and measured. No revenue forecast is credible before paid transactions exist.

## Operational constraints

The founder is a minor. An adult must handle contracts, payment accounts, tax obligations and any regulated data-processing commitments that cannot legally be held by a minor. Use only sanitized, authorized data. Do not accept credentials or production access.
