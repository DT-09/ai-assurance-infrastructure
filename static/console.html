Assurance lifecycle

From evidence to action
01 · REGISTER
Asset identity established

System, version, environment, model/framework and ownership become durable control-plane records.

02 · EVALUATE
Evidence submitted

Reliability, security, behavior, policy and operational evidence is attached to the exact asset version.

03 · COMPUTE
Trust state calculated

Policy rules produce an explicit assurance state with transition history and provenance.

04 · ENFORCE
Execution decision issued

Deployment and runtime controls consume the trust state and return allow, review or deny.
