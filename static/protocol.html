Specification

Protocol contract

The protocol defines the interoperable representation of an AI system's identity and current assurance state. Implementations may be independent; verification must be deterministic.

01

Identity

Identify the AI asset, immutable version, environment and ownership context.

02

Authority

Declare the capabilities, tools, data and actions relevant to operation.

03

Evidence

Bind machine-verifiable evidence and provenance to the asset version.

04

Trust state

Represent the current assurance posture and its policy basis.

05

Enforcement

Express the operational decision produced from assurance state.

06

Integrity

Protect message integrity with SHA-256 over canonical JSON content.
